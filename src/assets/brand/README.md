# Currenc Connect identity

`symbol.svg` is the scalable, transparent vector adaptation of the double-C concept approved by Benny. The pine and sage C forms represent the company and its shareholders connecting through Currenc Connect. It is used with the HTML Currenc / CONNECT wordmark throughout the portal and as the favicon. No background rectangle or generated review-sheet text is embedded.

Approved concept reference: `exec-d212ccf3-b74c-4f8b-9cb8-e4485eb5ae37.png` from the November 2026 design conversation. The reference is a concept board; the checked-in SVG is a hand-built web asset, not a pixel-exact crop. Do not use the entire concept board as a logo, or replace the symbol with an unrelated generated mark.
